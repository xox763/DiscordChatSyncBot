Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Information We Process

HW Chat Sync for Discord operates on a minimal-data principle to perform real-time chat relay services:

  • In-Game Chat Data: In-game usernames and text messages sent within the guild chat for transmission purposes.
  • Discord Technical Identifiers: Discord Guild (Server) IDs, Channel IDs, and Webhook URLs necessary for routing messages.

2. How We Use Data

All collected and transmitted data is used exclusively to relay chat messages in real-time between the game environment and your designated Discord channels. We do NOT sell, trade, or monetize your chat content or user data.

3. Data Storage & Retention

  • No Permanent Storage: Chat messages are processed in-memory for real-time queueing and forwarding. They are not permanently stored on our servers or databases.
  • Temporary Buffers: Messages are held in temporary memory buffers (queue) only for the duration required to satisfy rate-limit throttle requirements (typically seconds) and are immediately discarded afterward.

4. Third-Party Infrastructure

Message traffic may pass through third-party infrastructure (such as Cloudflare Workers or Edge Proxies) to ensure service stability, rate-limit protection, and secure routing. These services process data strictly in accordance with standard web delivery protocols.

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or data handling, please reach out via our official GitHub repository issue tracker.
